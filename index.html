The road to 1500, level by level

Every student gets a plan based on their current level. We track progress with monthly rating checks and game reviews.

Level 1 · Beginner (0–600)
  • Moves, rules, checkmate patterns
  • Opening principles
  • Fun puzzle games
Level 2 · Club (600–1000)
  • Tactics: forks, pins, skewers
  • Basic endgames
  • Own-game analysis
Level 3 · Tournament (1000–1300)
  • Opening repertoire
  • Strategy and planning
  • Clock and online tournament practice
Level 4 · Rating 1500
  • Deep calculation
  • Advanced endgames
  • National and international event prep

Questions parents ask

My child has never played. Is that fine?

Yes. We start from the first move and keep lessons playful for young children.

How long to reach 1500?

It depends on age, practice time and starting level. After the free assessment we give a realistic timeline.

What do we need for class?

A phone, tablet or computer with internet, Zoom, and a free chess.com or lichess account.

Can I pay from the UK, USA, Poland or Europe?

Yes. Choose your region in the plans section. Pay in £, $, zł or € by bank transfer, PayPal, Wise, Revolut, Zelle, Venmo or BLIK. Message us on WhatsApp and we send the details for your method.

Are classes held at times that suit my country?

Yes. We teach evenings and weekends in UK, Poland and US time zones. Use the time converter above.
